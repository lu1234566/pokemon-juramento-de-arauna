# Remembrancers Base — English restoration

Status: English-only migration slice.

This slice restores the complete authored 1F–4F Remembrancers Base surface in the official English build while preserving the Magma Hideout event skeleton.

## Lower base — 30 blocks

### 1F: entry
The base is introduced as a place that stores copies of material HORIZON tried to remove. Members immediately establish that they do not all agree with LUZIA.

### 2F archive evidence
The lower archive contains:

- notebooks from M'BOI families;
- HORIZON material stamped as therapeutic records;
- blacked-out testimony;
- files recovered before disposal/incineration;
- ELIAS appearing in approvals while objections also survive;
- explicit warnings that documents are evidence, not complete truth.

Members cross-check names, dates and testimony rather than treating any single document as unquestionable.

### 2F internal ethics
The faction openly debates whether a stolen memory can ethically be returned without consent. Several REMEMBRANCERS support LUZIA because she confronts erasure while rejecting the idea that she is infallible.

## Upper base — 26 blocks

The RECORD-MATRIX is explained as a locator for displaced memory rather than a container. Sensor readings rise beyond scale as activation begins.

RAUL remains loyal to LUZIA because he saw families erased from records, but admits fear about what she may do.

At the core:

- REMEMBRANCERS realize restoration is pulling thousands of memories at once;
- one explicitly admits HORIZON may be right about the risk;
- LUZIA activates the RECORD-MATRIX believing it can return what the ARCHIVE tore away;
- the current stops following the MATRIX and begins pulling everything;
- after the player battle, LUZIA follows the signals toward PORTO DO SAL.

## Technical contract

Two English wrappers reuse the existing anchor/signature-checked renderers:

- 30 lower-base blocks across 1F, 2F_1R and 2F_2R;
- 26 upper-base blocks across 3F_1R, 3F_2R and 4F.

All visible segments are at most 32 characters. The six source maps are backed up and restored transactionally by the English build.

Trainer IDs/parties, RAUL/Tabitha slot, LUZIA/Maxie event slot, activation sequence, objects, movements, flags, warps, saves, geometry and art are untouched.
