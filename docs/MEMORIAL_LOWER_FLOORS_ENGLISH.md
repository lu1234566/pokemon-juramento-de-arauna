## 1F — arrival

The first floor establishes ordinary memorial practice rather than faction exposition:

- a caretaker gives the unchanged CLEANSE TAG and distinguishes protection from wild POKéMON from dealing with grief;
- a visitor reports that CIRO came through silently and read every plaque in sight;
- another visitor speaks the name of a lost ZIGZAGOON aloud and admits that both speaking and silence can hurt differently.

Ciro remains off-screen. His behavior continues the fracture established on Route 121 without creating a new object or forcing a confrontation.

## 2F — people inside the memorial

The second floor keeps the existing trainer/event structure but rewrites the surface around why people actually come here:

- flowers left every year for a lost SKITTY;
- visitors who realize catching rare POKéMON is inappropriate in the moment;
- a young couple using humor and a battle to manage nervousness;
- a caretaker family that treats names as living oral memory;
- a frightened trainer learning that fear itself deserves attention.

DONA ZILA is referenced through the idea that a name changes as different people carry it, without becoming less true.
