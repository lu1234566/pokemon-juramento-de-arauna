Status: English-only migration slice.

This slice restores the complete visible Space Center replacement as MISSOES DO CEU: ordinary 1F operations, the REMEMBRANCER occupation and the 2F confrontation around the regional uplink.

## 1F — public center under occupation

The ground floor remains recognizably a working space center:

- launch windows and launch counts continue during the crisis;
- scientists talk about precision, meteor showers and Arauna's observation history;
- civilians retain ordinary curiosity about rockets, space and POKéMON;
- the existing stone/Sun Stone gift flow is preserved.

The occupation is not presented as harmless. REMEMBRANCERS admit that civilians also depend on the regional uplink and that taking over a public center is ethically uncomfortable even if they fear HORIZON's alternative.

The uplink is clarified as infrastructure that carries commands rather than memories. A remote sync key could trigger BOND sensors across Arauna.

## 2F — synchronization conflict

The upper floor establishes the actual dispute:

- HORIZON could connect the regional uplink to the LIVING ARCHIVE and extend commands across Arauna;
- LUZIA wants to broadcast M'BOI evidence and destroy the synchronization key before returning the network;
- RAUL supports her plan while openly wishing there were another way;
- SEU BENTO argues that preventing HORIZON control does not authorize REMEMBRANCERS to seize civilian infrastructure;
- several REMEMBRANCERS independently reach the same concern.

After the unchanged double-battle sequence, LUZIA accepts the contradiction: taking a network to stop others controlling it is still taking the network. She withdraws and resolves to circulate the M'BOI evidence another way.
