# Memorial dos Nomes — 3F to 6F English surface

Status: English-only narrative continuation.

This slice continues the Memorial dos Nomes climb from the already-authored lower floors through the former Mt. Pyre 3F–6F maps.

## Narrative progression

The floors move away from generic ghost-story dialogue and deepen the memorial's core idea without turning every trainer into a lore lecturer:

- 3F asks whether care can become ownership and whether certainty is enough to justify a choice;
- the existing Gabrielle Match Call arc focuses on how memory can change a person without becoming their master;
- 4F reframes a student's devotion to a dead teacher as grief rather than a debt that must be repaid through endless improvement;
- 5F moves from horror-as-scenery to the discomfort created when every loss has a name;
- 6F questions imagined voices and echoes, separating attention from proof.

## Ciro

On 6F, CEDRIC reports that CIRO passed on the stairs asking which plaque held his father's name. No one could answer him. Ciro continued climbing, angry at the question rather than at the person who failed to answer it.

Ciro remains off-screen, preserving the existing object layout while making his approach to the summit increasingly personal.

## Technical contract

The renderer changes 29 text blocks across four source maps:

- 13 on 3F;
- 3 on 4F;
- 3 on 5F;
- 10 on 6F.

Emerald's historically swapped 4F/5F script labels are deliberately preserved. Structural masking rejects non-dialogue edits, and all visible segments are at most 32 characters.

Trainer IDs/parties, Gabrielle and Valerie Match Calls/rematches, object placement, warps, saves, geometry and art are untouched.
