# Route 119 — Ciro and Seu Bento in English

Status: English-only narrative continuation.

This slice replaces the repeated Portuguese Route 119 rival surface with a single coherent English scene while preserving both internal May/Brendan branches.

## Ciro

Ciro's Route 119 voice now reinforces his central conflict:

- he rejects the idea that moving forward means forgetting;
- he refuses to let loss decide every future choice;
- he gives the unchanged FLY HM as a practical challenge to the protagonist;
- his defense of HORIZON is framed around efficiency and motion rather than cartoon villainy.

Both player-gender branches display equivalent Ciro dialogue even though the Emerald trainer IDs remain `TRAINER_MAY_*` / `TRAINER_BRENDAN_*` internally.

## Seu Bento

The unchanged Scott event slot now displays SEU BENTO. He sees Ciro moving too fast and gives the protagonist a thematic counterpoint: speed matters, but so does knowing what one is running toward.

The later Fortree/Mata do Meio call is also rendered as SEU BENTO in English.

- `INSTITUTO DAS AGUAS` -> `WEATHER INSTITUTE`;
